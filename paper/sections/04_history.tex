\subsection{Write side: in-place overwrite}
\label{sec:hist-write}

At lag 0, where staleness plays no role, B1 (in-place store) answers \nm{tab2a/gemma4/B1_sync/KU_past}\%, \nm{tab2a/glm-5.3/B1_sync/KU_past}\% and \nm{tab2a/gpt-6-luna/B1_sync/KU_past}\% of KU-past questions (gemma4, glm-5.3, gpt-6-luna), and DeltaWAL-Versioned (append-only store, empty WAL) \nm{tab2a/gemma4/deltawal_versioned/KU_past}\%, \nm{tab2a/glm-5.3/deltawal_versioned/KU_past}\% and \nm{tab2a/gpt-6-luna/deltawal_versioned/KU_past}\% (Table~\ref{tab:history}a). With \nm{datasets/heldout138/KU_past} questions the intervals are wide, but they do not overlap for any reader. KU-current accuracy is unaffected (\nm{tab2a/glm-5.3/B1_sync/KU_current}\% vs.\ \nm{tab2a/glm-5.3/deltawal_versioned/KU_current}\% for glm-5.3).

A failure analysis confirms the mechanism. For the two cloud readers we took every KU question that B1 answered wrongly and the DeltaWAL prompt at lag 1 answered correctly and assigned one cause by inspecting store and prompt: \nm{forensics/q1_combined/a_destructive_overwrite_past/comparative_Q_on_a_key_the_update_overwrote} past or comparative questions on a key the update overwrote, \nm{forensics/q1_combined/b_unordered_missed_merge_old_new_under_different_keys} missed merges, \nm{forensics/q1_combined/c_lossy_extraction} lossy extractions, \nm{forensics/q1_combined/e_reader_hedged/abstained_with_current_value_present} hedging answers and \nm{forensics/q1_combined/f_empty_reader_output} empty output. On the overwrite cases B1 is right on \nm{tab2a/destructive_cases_combined/mutable/correct} of \nm{tab2a/destructive_cases_combined/mutable/n} and DeltaWAL-Versioned on \nm{tab2a/destructive_cases_combined/versioned/correct} of \nm{tab2a/destructive_cases_combined/versioned/n}. The DeltaWAL prompt's CAS store also answers them at lag 1, but only because the update has not committed yet: before the lag-1 cutoff, \nm{forensics/q5_versions_before_lag1_cutoff/ku_questions_where_an_evidence_key_has_2plus_committed_versions} of \nm{datasets/heldout138/KU} KU questions have an evidence key with two committed versions. Asynchrony preserves history only until the update commits; afterwards a CAS store overwrites like B1.

\subsection{Read side: masking}
\label{sec:hist-read}

Shadow keeps the store intact but drops stored facts from the prompt when a newer WAL sentence is similar to them. Table~\ref{tab:history}b sweeps $\theta$ for gemma4 over five async cells (lags 1, 3 and 5 at $p_\text{ooo}=0$; lags 1 and 3 at $p_\text{ooo}=\nm{tab2b/pooled_cells/1/1}$); a lower $\theta$ masks more. KU-past accuracy rises monotonically as masking is relaxed, from \nm{tab2b/threshold_0.60/KU_past}\% at the most aggressive threshold through \nm{tab2b/threshold_0.72/KU_past}\% at the frozen threshold to \nm{tab2b/threshold_off/KU_past}\% with masking off, while KU-current stays between \nm{tab2b/threshold_0.72/KU_current}\% and \nm{tab2b/threshold_0.70/KU_current}\%. Masking buys nothing on current-value questions and costs past-value questions in proportion to how much it masks; the most and least aggressive settings have non-overlapping intervals (\nmci{tab2b/threshold_0.60/KU_past} vs.\ \nmci{tab2b/threshold_off/KU_past}). The frozen $\theta$ maximised correct masks under a cap on false masks on the dev split, a criterion that cannot see this cost. Figure~\ref{fig:destructive}a puts the two kinds of erasure side by side: write-side overwrite (B1 and \memzero{} sync) and read-side masking (Shadow) give KU-past accuracies between \nm{fig3/ku_past/B1_sync/glm-5.3}\% and \nm{fig3/ku_past/Mem0_sync/gpt-6-luna}\%, and DeltaWAL-Versioned at lag 1, which does neither, \nm{fig3/ku_past/deltawal_versioned/gemma4}\%--\nm{fig3/ku_past/deltawal_versioned/glm-5.3}\%.

\subsection{Store integrity under reordering}
\label{sec:hist-reorder}

At $p_\text{ooo}=\nm{tab2b/pooled_cells/1/1}$ and lag 1, LWW leaves \nm{tab2c/lag1/B2_async/corrupted_keys} of \nm{tab2c/lag1/B2_async/updated_keys} updated keys (\nm{tab2c/lag1/B2_async/oocr_pct}\%) at an older value than the newest committed one (\nm{tab2c/lag3/B2_async/oocr_pct}\% at lag 3); the CAS fence leaves none, rejecting \nm{tab2c/lag1/deltawal_prompt/cas_rejections} late writes (Table~\ref{tab:history}c). Corruption rarely reaches an evidence key: in a deterministic lag-0 replay with no model calls, \nm{forensics/q4_lag0_p03/questions_with_corrupted_evidence_key} questions had one. For question answering, reordering matters far less than staleness. The fence does not address \emph{missed merges}, where the facts from a KU question's update session share no key with its earlier evidence: \nm{tab2c/lag1/B2_async/questions_with_missed_merge} of \nm{tab2c/lag1/B2_async/n_questions} KU questions (\nm{tab2c/lag1/B2_async/missed_merge_pct}\%) at lag 1, identically under LWW and CAS, because the cause is extraction, not the write policy. A keyed store cannot supersede a value it does not recognise as the same entity; keeping both values and ordering them by epoch can, as in the \nm{forensics/q1_combined/b_unordered_missed_merge_old_new_under_different_keys} missed-merge cases of the failure analysis, too few to claim this in general.
